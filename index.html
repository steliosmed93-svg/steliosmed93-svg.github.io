Frequently asked questions

If your question isn't here, just ask in the form.

Which type of review do I need?

It depends on your question. A literature review explains a topic or builds an argument, a scoping review maps all the evidence on a topic, and a systematic review answers a focused question, often with a meta-analysis. The free assessment includes a recommendation.

Is the first assessment really free?

Yes. You receive confidential feedback within 48 hours, with no obligation to continue.

Do you write the review for me?

I provide methodological support, training and expert input so that the work remains yours and meets your institution's rules on academic integrity. The scope is agreed with you in advance.

Is my project kept confidential?

Yes. Your topic, data and drafts are never shared with anyone else.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the type of review and the stages you need help with. After the free assessment you receive a clear quote before any work begins.

Will I work only with Dr. Chatziioannou?

Dr. Chatziioannou leads and personally supervises every project, supported by his team of experienced researchers. This means stages that need two independent reviewers, such as screening and data extraction, are done properly.

Can you help in Greek?

Yes. Support is available in English and Greek.
